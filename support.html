Common Questions

Does Social Mirror require social account login?

No. Social Mirror uses screenshots you choose to upload. It does not require your Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, dating app, or other social account login.

Can Social Mirror guarantee results?

No. Social Mirror gives profile feedback and improvement recommendations, but it cannot guarantee matches, followers, recruiter responses, or other outcomes.

How do I manage subscriptions?

If you purchased through Apple, you can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple account subscription settings.
